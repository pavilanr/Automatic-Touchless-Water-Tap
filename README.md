# Automatic-Touchless-Water-Tap
A sensor-based automatic touchless water tap that detects hand movement and controls a water pump to dispense water automatically. Designed to reduce water wastage, improve hygiene, and demonstrate practical electronics automation.
# 💧 Automatic Touchless Water Tap

## 📌 Project Overview
The Automatic Touchless Water Tap is an electronics-based project designed to provide water without physically touching a tap. It uses a sensor to detect the presence of a hand and automatically activates the water supply.

This project helps reduce water wastage, improves hygiene, and provides a simple automation solution.

## 🎯 Objectives
- Develop a touchless water dispensing system.
- Reduce water wastage.
- Improve hygiene by avoiding physical contact.
- Understand sensor-based automation and electronic control circuits.

## 🔧 Components Required
- IR Sensor
- Mini DC Water Pump
- Battery / DC Power Supply
- Transistor or Relay Module
- Connecting Wires
- Water Tubing
- Breadboard

## ⚙️ Working Principle
1. The IR sensor detects the presence of a hand near the tap.
2. The sensor sends a signal to the control circuit.
3. The control circuit activates the water pump.
4. Water flows through the tube.
5. When the hand is removed, the pump stops automatically.
